About Qiskit Fall Fest

Discover the fascinating world of quantum computing through hands-on workshops, expert-led training sessions, and collaborative hackathons.

What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

Qiskit Fall Fest is the world's largest collection of student-run quantum computing events, bringing together passionate quantum enthusiasts from around the globe. As one of the selected international universities, Cyber University is proud to host this prestigious event.

Our event celebrates the remarkable advances quantum computing has made over the past year, featuring collaboration between experienced quantum scientists and budding young quantum enthusiasts. Together, we'll explore cutting-edge quantum technologies through interactive workshops and challenging hackathons.

Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced developer, this event offers something for everyone. Join us as we dive deep into the quantum realm and discover the limitless possibilities of quantum computing.

Hands-on Workshops

Interactive sessions with quantum computing fundamentals and practical applications.

Expert Training

Learn from IBM Quantum experts and industry professionals.

Hackathon Challenge

Compete in exciting quantum computing challenges and win prizes.

Community Building

Connect with like-minded quantum enthusiasts and build lasting relationships.
