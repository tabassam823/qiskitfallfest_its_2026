What is Qiskit Fall Fest 2026?

Qiskit Fall Fest is the largest student-driven quantum computing event series in the world, organized across leading universities globally in partnership with IBM Quantum.

In 2026, Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) was proudly selected as a primary host in Indonesia to bring interactive quantum learning, real-device cloud access, and a supportive research community directly to students and faculty.
