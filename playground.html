📖 Quick Guide to Quantum Logic Gates

Pauli-X Gate (NOT / Bit Flip)

Flips the computational basis state: $|0\rangle \to |1\rangle$ (Spin Up $\to$ Spin Down) and vice versa. It acts as the quantum NOT gate.

Hadamard Gate (H)

Transforms a basis state into an equal superposition $|+\rangle = \frac{|0\rangle + |1\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}$, yielding a 50/50 measurement probability.

CNOT Gate (Controlled-X)

A 2-qubit gate essential for creating quantum entanglement. Flips the target qubit if and only if the control qubit is in state $|1\rangle$.
